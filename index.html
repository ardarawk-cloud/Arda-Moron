01 / ABOUT

Music first.
Always evolving.

Arda Moron is a Bali-based DJ and creative entrepreneur active behind the decks since 2005.

Built on years of club culture, private events, nightlife, and creative projects, his sets are intentionally fluid: all genre when the room calls for it, but always pulled toward house music and its deeper, percussive, soulful, and driving edges.
